PROPOSAL PRESENTATION OUTLINE — adapt to the time allotted on LEARN

These are suggested sections, not a new slide-count or assessment requirement.
1. Question and intended decision (A): title, unit, target/estimand, why the question matters.
2. Research connection (A): motivating paper claim, reused method and scoped contribution.
3. Data feasibility (B): source/access, sample, label provenance, timing, quality risks and evidence inspected so far.
4. Traditional baseline (C): algorithm/specification, assumptions, fit scope and diagnostics.
5. LLM/agent treatment (D): context, tools, output schema, finite budget, audit and information control.
6. Evaluation plan (B/E): partition/estimand, primary metric, uncertainty/repeats, coverage and freeze rule.
7. Feasibility and milestones (F): planned artifact, responsibilities, resources, risks and fallback.
8. Questions for feedback: decisions needing resolution before implementation.

Mark plans, preliminary measurements and not-run components distinctly. Use diagrams/tables only when they explain the design. Do not include invented results or secrets. Tie each empirical statement to an artifact or source.
